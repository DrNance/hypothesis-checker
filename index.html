Welcome to the Hypothesis Checker

This activity will guide you through checking two research hypotheses before you submit your first draft. We'll look at each hypothesis together — one question at a time — and give you feedback along the way.

You'll check whether each hypothesis is appropriate for a t-test, whether your study is feasible, and whether it meets ethical guidelines. If something needs adjusting, you'll have the chance to revise right here.

At the end, you'll be able to download a summary of your approved hypotheses as proof of completion.

Take your time. There are no trick questions — just a chance to think through your ideas carefully before they affect your grade.
